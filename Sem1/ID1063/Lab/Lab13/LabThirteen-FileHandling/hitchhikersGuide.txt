The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy is a remarkable book. It has been compiled and 
recompiled many times over the years and under many different editorships. It contains contributions from
countless numbers of travellers and researchers. The introduction begins like this: "Space," it says, "is big.
Really big. You just won't believe how vastly hugely mindbogglingly big it is. I mean
you may think it is a long way down the road to the chemist, but that's just peanuts to space. Listen..."
and so on.
