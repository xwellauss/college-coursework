
Number Theory is the study of integers.

#### Division
Let a, b are integers and $a \not = 0$. We say *a divides b* if there exists an integer d such that: $b = ad$. And if $b \not = 0$, then $|a| \le |b|$.

**Notation**: $a | b$ denotes a divides b. If a does not divide b, we write $a \not | b$ 

**Division Theorem**
For any integer, $a \not = 0$ and any positive integer b, there exist unique integers q and r, such that: b = aq + r and $0 \le r < b$.

b is the divisor.
a is the dividend.
q is the quotient.
r is the remainder.
