### Syllabus
- **Part 1: Primary Modules**
	- Logic & Proofs
	- Basic Structures - Sets, Functions, Matrices
	- Number Theory & Cryptography
	- Induction & Recursion
	- Counting Techniques
- **Part 2: Advanced Topics**
	- Discreet Probability
	- Recurrence & Generating Functions
	- Graph Theory
	- *Relations*
	- *Boolean Algebra*
