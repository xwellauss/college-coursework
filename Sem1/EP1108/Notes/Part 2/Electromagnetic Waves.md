### Blackbody Radiation
- As temperature increases, the color of hot object changes approximately as Red — Orange — Yellow ....— Bluish-white
- Every object above absolute zero contains vibrating atoms and electrons.
- The accelerating charges emit electromagnetic radiation, every hot object continuously emits EM waves.
